# ♿ Inclusive Voice Design & Accessibility Guide

> **Project:** SkillForge — AI-Powered Alexa Skill Builder via MCP  
> **Philosophy:** *"Voice interfaces should work for everyone, not just power users."*

---

## Overview

Voice is inherently the most natural human-computer interface, yet millions of users—including the elderly, hearing-impaired, non-native speakers, and neurodivergent individuals—frequently struggle with rapid, monotone, or poorly formatted Alexa responses.

SkillForge embeds **Inclusive Voice Design (IVD)** directly into its Bedrock Claude 3 code generation pipeline. Every skill generated by SkillForge automatically adheres to accessibility best practices.

---

## 5 Pillars of Accessible Voice Design in SkillForge

### 1. Automatic SSML Structuring
Generated Lambda handlers avoid plain text speech and format responses with Speech Synthesis Markup Language (SSML):
```javascript
const speakOutput = `<speak>
    <prosody rate="95%">Got it.</prosody>
    <break time="300ms"/>
    I logged 500 milliliters of water.
    <break time="200ms"/>
    You are at 1,500 milliliters towards your goal.
</speak>`;
```
- `<break time="300ms"/>`: Inserts natural cognitive pauses between ideas, allowing auditory processing time.
- `<prosody rate="95%">`: Slightly decelerated cadence improves comprehension for elderly listeners.
- `<emphasis level="moderate">`: Highlights crucial numbers and status changes without shouting.

### 2. Multi-Speed Response Configuration
Hearing-impaired and elderly users can toggle response speech rates from `80%` to `120%` in the skill's persistent settings:
- **80% - 90% (Elderly / Cognitive Accessibility):** Slower cadence, wider pauses.
- **100% (Standard):** Balanced natural speech.
- **110% - 120% (Power Users / Screen Reader Users):** Rapid review.

### 3. Phonetic Pronunciation Tags (`<phoneme>`)
For medical terms, metric units, cultural greetings, and regional terms, SkillForge automatically generates IPA phonetic annotations:
```xml
<speak>
    Welcome to <phoneme alphabet="ipa" ph="ˈskɪlˌfɔːrdʒ">SkillForge</phoneme>!
</speak>
```

### 4. Multi-Language & Regional Accent Support
SkillForge generates localized skills across 5 major global languages and dialects:
- **English:** `en-US`, `en-GB`, `en-CA`, `en-AU`, `en-IN`
- **Hindi:** `hi-IN` (Hinglish and Devanagari prompts)
- **Spanish:** `es-ES`, `es-US`, `es-MX`
- **German:** `de-DE`
- **Japanese:** `ja-JP`

When generating Hindi skills, SkillForge incorporates natural conversational markers:
```xml
<speak>
    नमस्ते! आपका पानी का लॉग रिकॉर्ड कर लिया गया है।
</speak>
```

### 5. Multimodal Screen Accessibility (APL)
For Echo Show and Fire TV multimodal devices, SkillForge generates high-contrast Alexa Presentation Language (APL) layouts:
- Minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 (WCAG AA).
- Large 48pt+ typography for distance viewing.
- Synchronized speech and on-screen text highlighting for dual-channel learning.
